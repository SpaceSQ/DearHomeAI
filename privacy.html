Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Dear Home AI ("Dear Home", "we", "our") is built on a simple promise: the house lives on your device, and your conversations do not leave it. This policy explains what little we do process and what we never see.

1. What stays on your device

Dear Home AI is 100% on-device and offline-first. Your chat history, space notes, memories, soul profile, and evolution data are stored in the app's local sandbox on your iPhone (Application Support/DearHome/) and in Apple App Group containers that belong to you on this device. None of this content is uploaded to our servers or any cloud storage we operate.

2. Sign in with Apple

Sign in with Apple is handled by Apple. We receive the Apple user identifier that Apple generates for you, and — on your first sign-in only — the name and email you choose to share. We use these only to bind your on-device profile to your Apple account so that signing out and back in returns to the same content.

When you sign in, the one-time authorization code Apple issues is exchanged for a refresh token. This token is stored in your device's secure Keychain and is used solely to revoke access if you choose to delete your account. Apple's own privacy practices apply to your Apple ID.

3. Subscriptions & payments

Subscriptions (Free / Pro / Max) are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We never see or store your payment card details, billing address, or transaction history. Purchase and refund policies are governed by Apple's App Store terms.

4. Apple Home & local family sharing

With your permission, Dear Home AI reads your HomeKit homes, rooms, accessories, and scenes so the house can talk about them. This version only suggests changes and does not control devices.

The family queue shares household facts between your own devices over the local network (Bonjour / MQTT). These facts travel device-to-device and are not stored on our servers.

5. What we do not collect

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • We do not run third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not upload your conversations, memories, or household facts to a cloud we control.

6. Account deletion

Deleting your account erases every room, memory, family note, and preference on this iPhone, revokes the Apple refresh token, and returns the app to a fresh, signed-out state. Signing out (as opposed to deleting) keeps your local profile on the device.

7. Children's privacy

Dear Home AI is a general-purpose companion and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the revised version on this page.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: support@dearhomeai.space2.world or GitHub Issues.
