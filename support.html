Frequently asked questions

Is Dear Home AI really offline?

Yes. Chat, space notes, and memories are stored in your iPhone's local sandbox and are not uploaded to a cloud. Sign in with Apple and subscriptions are the only flows that talk to Apple's services.

The house says no model is available. How do I get it talking?

The on-device model is optional. Without a local model, the house falls back to a rule-based dialogue engine that still answers, suggests, and remembers. To use a local model, place a compatible GGUF in the shared models folder or in Application Support/DearHome/models/.

What is the nightly memory letter?

At 00:00 each day, the house distills the day's conversation into a Markdown (.md) memory letter. Pro and Max unlock reading and exporting the full letters.

How do I restore a subscription on a new device?

Open Settings → Subscription → Help & Pricing and tap Restore Purchases. Your subscription is tied to your Apple ID.

How do refunds work?

Payments are processed by Apple. To request a refund, use Apple's Report a Problem flow — we cannot issue refunds from inside the app.

What happens when I delete my account?

Deleting the account wipes every room, memory, and family note on this iPhone, revokes the Apple sign-in token, and returns you to a fresh, signed-out state.
